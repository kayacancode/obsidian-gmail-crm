# Gmail CRM 0.9.2

Gmail CRM 0.9.2 adds an explainable, temporary relevance layer to the existing relationship explorer while preserving the relationship graph underneath it.

## What is new

- **Balanced theme heat:** soft labeled fields and restrained person pulses show which subjects are active now without replacing relationship strength, paths, photographs, labels, or evidence. The My mind, Firm, Public momentum, and Off lenses keep their evidence boundaries explicit, and Off restores the graph without heat.
- **Why-now evidence:** selecting heat opens the evidence behind it, including reasons, score components, dates, freshness, extraction method, and available correction, pin, mute, expiry, or retrieval controls. Theme heat is advisory context and may be incomplete or wrong; it is not verified expertise, employment, intent, closeness, or consent.
- **Connector rings:** solid rings identify documented graph bridges and dashed rings identify inferred structure. Rings are scored separately from heat and relationship strength, and they never establish willingness or ability to make an introduction.
- **Local Granola and Obsidian signals:** bounded summaries, decisions, action items, and key themes can contribute through the existing local vault processing and authenticated graph push. The service does not add a Granola OAuth connection or silently crawl a vault; source material stays local unless the user explicitly pushes the bounded snapshot.
- **Explicit bounded email retrieval:** Retrieve more context starts only after a preview and confirmation for a selected person or theme and inbox. A request covers 30 days by default or 90 days when selected, with hard caps of 50 matching messages and 1 MB of decoded text. Bodies are processed ephemerally, and raw bodies are not retained in SQL, KV, graph responses, or logs.
- **Opt-in public sources:** a person/company URL or RSS/Atom feed is fetched only after preview and confirmation. Redirects, network targets, content types, response size, and time are bounded, and every public assertion keeps source provenance.

## Privacy and control

Private, firm, and public evidence remain separate. Firm views use only explicitly firm-shareable evidence; Public momentum uses only public evidence; My mind may use the evidence authorized for that owner. Tenant storage and authenticated routes remain owner-scoped. Public evidence never silently becomes private evidence, and sharing an assertion does not automatically share its underlying evidence.

Gmail CRM and People Graph never send outreach, email, introduction requests, or CRM actions automatically. They present inspectable context for the user to review and act on.

## Updating

Existing BRAT users can update `kayacancode/obsidian-gmail-crm` through BRAT. For a manual update, replace the plugin's `main.js`, `manifest.json`, and `styles.css` with the three release assets, preserving existing plugin data and settings, then reload the plugin.
