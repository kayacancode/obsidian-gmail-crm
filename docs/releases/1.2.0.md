# People Data Sync 1.2.0

Granola meeting notes now say who was there, as links to their people pages.

## Who was in each meeting

- Each Granola note gets a `people:` property: links to the people pages of the attendees, then plain emails for attendees without a page. Attendees are found the same way as for the `## Granola meetings` sections in 1.1.0 (the matching calendar meeting first, else the note's attendee emails). You, shared calendars and automated senders such as noreply addresses are left out.
- Only that property is written. The rest of the note, including its body and every other frontmatter line, stays exactly as it was, and nothing is written when the list hasn't changed.
- When Granola Sync rewrites a note after Granola updates it, the property is put back automatically.

## People pages for meeting attendees

- With **Create contact notes** on, attendees without a people page get one, but only when their name is known (a Gmail contact name or the calendar invite's display name). Pages are never named after an email address. Attendees without a known name are listed by email in `people:` until one is.
- Existing pages and aliases are checked first, so nobody gets a second page, and an existing file is never overwritten.
- Calendar sync now keeps attendees' display names from invites.

Gmail sync, scoring, saved data and the signed release process are unchanged.
