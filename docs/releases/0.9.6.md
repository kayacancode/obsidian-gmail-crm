# Gmail CRM 0.9.6

Relationship mapping now runs locally after each successful manual or scheduled Gmail sync. It updates people-page relationship sections and saves graph edges for People Intelligence without using AI. Generated relationship links no longer feed back into the next mapping pass, and unchanged pages keep their modification time.

The new **View people graph** command opens your configured People graph URL, or the hosted graph when no URL is set. Viewing does not upload vault data; use **Push people graph to web** when you want to publish a new snapshot.

This release keeps AI enrichment optional. Existing vault settings and contact data remain compatible.
